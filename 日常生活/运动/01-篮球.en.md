# Basketball

NBA live broadcast common words

- **REB**= Rebounds →**Rebounds**(total rebounds, offensive rebounds OREB + defensive rebounds DREB)
- **AST**= Assists →**assists**(passing the ball directly contributes to teammates scoring)
- **STL**= Steals →**steals**
- **BLK**= Blocks →**Blocks / Blocks**
- **TO**= Turnovers →**Turnovers**(TOV is also written in many places)
- **PF**= Personal Fouls →**Personal foul**(NBA players have left the game with a total of 6 fouls)
- **PTS (Points)**:**scored**, how many points did the player score in total statsdmz.n...
- **FG (Field Goals)**:**sports shooting**(two points + three points are counted, the general format: hit - shot, such as 7-14, 7-14);**FG%**is the field goal percentage
- **3P (Three-Pointers)**:**three-pointer**, also hit - shot;**3P%**three-point shooting rate
- **FT (Free Throws)**:**free throw**, hit - shot;**FT%**free throw shooting percentage
- **+/- (Plus-Minus, plus or minus value)**: How many points the team outperformed its opponents during the time the player was on the court.
