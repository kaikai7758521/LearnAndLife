# Paddle electric propeller circuit design notes
> applicable scenarios: SUP paddle board with external electric propeller, supports wireless remote control, stepless speed regulation, forward/reverse, and steering; distinguish different battery solutions for circuit planning, focusing on safety and practical operation precautions.
> ⚠️ Important statement: This design is for learning reference only. There are risks of water intrusion, short circuit, and fire in waterborne electrical equipment. The finished product must be waterproof and have safety redundancy.

## 1. Sorting out functional requirements
1. wireless remote control: remote control ↔ receiving module communication
2. motor control: forward, reverse, stepless speed regulation (PWM)
3. steering control: the steering gear drives the rudder blades to achieve directional control
4. power supply adaptation: supports 3S/4S lithium battery (model aircraft lithium battery), 12V lead-acid/lithium iron phosphate solution
5. protection requirements: overcurrent, undervoltage, anti-reverse connection, emergency stop, waterproof isolation

> core architecture: **battery → power distribution/protection unit → ESC (drives main propulsion motor) + steering gear drive unit + remote control receiving module**

## 2. Overall circuit
```
动力电池组
├─ 主保护支路：保险丝 → 防反接模块
├─ BEC降压模块：输出5V，给接收机、舵机供电
├─ 水下无刷电调ESC：接收PWM信号，驱动推进无刷电机（支持正反转）
└─ 防水舵机：接收PWM信号，控制转向舵叶

遥控器（发射机） ===2.4G无线===> 接收机
接收机通道分配：
- CH1：油门通道（速度控制，中位停机，上推前进，下拉后退）
- CH2：转向通道（左右方向控制）
```

> Description: Give priority to **, the underwater brushless ESC that supports forward and reverse rotation,**; ordinary model aircraft ESCs do not support reverse by default, so do not mix them.

## 3. Detailed explanation of sub-module circuit
### 3.1 wireless remote control module
Recommended solution: 2.4G model aircraft remote control (Fusi FS-i6, Ledi AT9S, etc.)
- transmitter: joystick, all the way to throttle, all the way to steering
- receiving end: the receiver outputs standard model aircraft PWM signal (50Hz, 1000~2000us pulse width)
- power supply: The working voltage of the receiver is 4.8~6V, powered by BEC. **is prohibited from directly connecting to lithium battery high voltage**
- wiring:
  - throttle channel → ESC signal line (white=signal, red=5V, black=GND)
  - steering channel → steering gear signal line

> ⚠️ Choose one of the ESC’s built-in BEC and external BEC. Do not supply dual power supplies to the receiver **at the same time, otherwise the receiver** will be burned.

### 3.2 propulsion motor drive unit (ESC electronic control)
Selection: **underwater brushless ESC, supports forward and reverse, waterproof model**
- input: power battery voltage
- output: three-phase line connected to brushless motor
- signal port: PWM input
- built-in functions: throttle curve setting, forward and reverse switching, brake setting, low voltage protection LVC
> wiring points:
> 1. The thick red and thick black wires of the ESC are connected to the battery main power supply; the three-phase thick wires are connected to the motor (replace any two phase wires and the motor will reverse direction)
> 2. Three pins of the signal line: GND, signal, 5V; if using an external BEC power supply receiver, you need to cut the red wire in the ESC signal line (cut off the built-in BEC output of the ESC)

### 3.3 steering servo unit
- model selection: high torque waterproof servo (special for underwater, ordinary plastic servo will be directly damaged by water), torque recommendation ≥15kg·cm
- power supply: 5~6V, sharing BEC power supply with the receiver
- control: PWM signal, pulse width 1000μs left rudder, 1500μs center, 2000μs right rudder
- Machinery: The rudder arm connecting rod is connected to the underwater rudder blade, and a mechanical limit is reserved to prevent stalling and burning the steering gear.

### 3.4 power supply and BEC step-down unit (focus, battery solution)
The function of > BEC is to reduce the high voltage of the power battery to 5V and provide power to the receiver and steering gear.

#### Plan A: 3S lithium battery (11.1V, fully charged 12.6V)
- battery: 3S model aircraft lithium battery, capacity 5Ah~20Ah, suitable for small and medium-sized paddle boards
- main circuit: 20A~40A fuse (according to motor power) + high-power anti-reverse connection MOS module
- BEC selection: DC-DC step-down module, input 7~18V, output 5V 3A or more (the steering gear starts with a large impact current)
- low voltage protection: ESC LVC setting 9.0V cut-off (single core 3.0V) to prevent over-discharge bulging

#### Plan B: 4S lithium battery (14.8V, fully charged 16.8V)
- battery: 4S lithium battery, stronger power and higher speed
- main circuit: 30A~60A fuse
- BEC selection: DC-DC step-down module, input 10~24V, output 5V 3A+, wide voltage version
- low voltage protection: LVC setting 12.0V cut-off (single core 3.0V)
> Note: 4S voltage is high, insulation requirements are higher, and the risk of leakage is greater

#### Solution C: 12V lead-acid/12V lithium iron phosphate (4 strings of lithium iron fully charged at 14.6V)
- battery: 12V lead-acid/lithium iron phosphate, low cost and good safety. The disadvantage is that it is large in size and weight.
- main circuit: Same as the 3S solution, the fuse is matched according to the rated current of the motor
- BEC: input 9~18V, output 5V
- low voltage threshold: lead-acid is recommended to shut down below 11V; lithium iron phosphate single string 2.5V protection

### 3.5 protection circuit list (required)
1. **anti-reverse connection**: High-power anti-reverse connection MOS module, it is not recommended to only use diodes (high current causes serious heat)
2. **Main fuse**: Close to the positive terminal of the battery, it will fuse immediately in case of short circuit
3. **Low voltage protection**: Priority ESC comes with LVC; if the budget is sufficient, add an independent low voltage alarm + relay to cut off the main circuit
4. **emergency stop switch**: waterproof boat type emergency stop, connected in series to the main positive circuit, one-button power-off when falling into the water
5. **Common ground**: All module GNDs must be connected together, otherwise the PWM signal will be abnormal, the servo will shake randomly, and the throttle will be out of control.

## 4. Complete wiring table
| device | positive pole of power supply | negative pole of power supply | signal line | remarks |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Power battery | → Fuse → Anti-reverse connection → ESC main positive | ESC main negative | - | Main power circuit |
| ESC | Main positive | Main negative | PWM signal → Receiver CH1 | Red wire cut (when external BEC is used) |
| Servo | BEC 5V | BEC GND | PWM signal → Receiver CH2 | Waterproof servo |
| receiver | BEC 5V | BEC GND | - | All signals GND common ground |
| BEC step-down | Battery main positive (after fuse) | Battery main negative | - | Output 5V to servo + receiver |

## 5. Circuit debugging steps
1. is powered off and connected. All GNDs are grounded. Check repeatedly whether there is a short circuit between the positive and negative poles.
Power on the receiver + BEC separately for 2. , bind the remote control, and test whether the left and right steering of the servo is smooth and the limit is normal.
3. disconnect the motor phase line, power on and calibrate the ESC throttle stroke (ESC calibration is a necessary step, otherwise the throttle will not be linear and there will be no reverse)
Connect 4. to the motor phase wires, test forward/reverse at low speed, and confirm that the steering and rocker directions are consistent; reverse any two phase wires of the motor.
5. tests emergency stop and low voltage alarm to confirm that the protection action is normal
6. has been fully debugged on land and will be tested in the water again.

## 6. Safety and practical precautions
### Electrical safety
1. is a high-humidity conductive environment on the water. All wiring is prioritized for welding + heat shrink tube sealing; waterproof aviation plugs are used for connectors, and ordinary Dupont wires are prohibited from being exposed and soaked in water.
It is strictly forbidden to squeeze or puncture the 2. lithium battery; check the battery shell for bulges and cracks before entering the water. It is recommended that the lithium battery be placed in an independent sealed waterproof battery compartment.
The 3. power loop and signal loop are routed separately, and the high-current main line is kept away from the signal line to reduce electromagnetic interference and avoid remote control loss.
The 4. ESC and BEC cannot be completely sealed, and the power devices generate heat during operation. It is preferable to use potting glue that can dissipate heat for waterproofing. If they are completely sealed without heat dissipation, they will overheat and burn.
5. Do not increase the size of the fuse at will. The fuse is the last short circuit protection.
The 6. remote control must turn on the loss-of-control protection: when the vehicle loses control, the throttle returns to zero and the servo returns to center to prevent the vehicle from running out of control.

### Mechanical and waterproof
1. ordinary servos and ordinary model aircraft ESCs **are not waterproof and** must be purchased for underwater use. Ordinary model aircraft components are easily damaged by water and can cause short circuit and fire.
The 2. propulsion motor and rudder blades are underwater. The cable outlet position is sealed and waterproof with a gland. Leave a margin at the bend of the cable to prevent it from being pulled and broken.
The 3. paddle board is a flexible carrier, which causes large vibrations during navigation. All wiring should be fixed against earthquakes to prevent joints from loosening and sparking.

### Usage specifications
1. is fully functional on land before launching into the water: forward, backward, speed adjustment, steering, emergency stop, remote control distance test.
Do not operate the 2. at full throttle for a long time, as the motor and ESC will overheat; the continuous power will be reduced in high temperature seasons.
After 3. comes ashore, flush the propeller with fresh water in time, dry it thoroughly, and check the joints for water seepage and corrosion.
It is strictly prohibited to use 4. at high speed in the main channel of the river and in the ship passage area; wear a life jacket at all times.

### Circuit pit avoidance list
- ❌ Error: Ordinary model aircraft ESCs are directly used for reversing; most of them can only rotate forward, and the tubes may explode when reversing.
- ❌ Error: ESC BEC and external BEC supply power to the receiver at the same time
- ❌ Error: The signal lines are not in the same ground, the servo is shaking, and the throttle is blowing.
- ❌ Error: The BEC current margin is too small, the servo pulls down the voltage instantly when turning the direction, and the receiver restarts out of control.
- ❌ Error: Failure protection is not set, and the motor continues to run after the remote control loses signal.

## 7. Optional upgrade expansion circuit
1. adds a voltage display meter: waterproof voltmeter to check battery voltage in real time
2. adds audible and visual alarm: low voltage buzzer, low voltage warning in advance
3. dual-channel independent relay: high-power model, independently controls the main motor power supply
4. has speed feedback with Hall sensor to achieve closed-loop constant speed control
