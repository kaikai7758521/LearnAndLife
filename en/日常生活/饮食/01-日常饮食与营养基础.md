# Daily diet and nutritional basics
> In a word: In daily diet, first ensure that the total amount of **is appropriate, enough protein, sufficient vegetables and fruits, and less processed food**. There is no need to chase some kind of "super food" or extreme diet.
> Note: The following is general nutrition science and does not constitute medical advice; for those who are pregnant, lactating, chronically ill, eating disorders, or taking special medications, please consult a doctor or registered dietitian.

## 1. Energy balance: the underlying logic of weight changes
```
体重变化 ≈ 摄入热量 - 消耗热量
消耗 = 基础代谢 BMR + 活动消耗 + 食物热效应
```
- Basal metabolism accounts for the majority, and muscle mass, weight, age, and gender will all affect it;
- Activity consumption includes walking, exercise, and NEAT (non-exercise activities);
- Weight loss requires **long-term small caloric deficit**, and weight gain requires a small surplus;
The - estimation formula is for reference only, and the true value needs to be calibrated using the weight trend of 2 to 4 weeks.

Experience value:
- Daily 300~500 kcal deficit, approximately 0.3~0.5 kg loss per week;
- If the gap is too large, it will easily lead to muscle loss, fatigue, and rebound;
- It is normal for weight to fluctuate 0.5~2 kg per day (water, salt, glycogen, defecation).

## 2. Three major nutrients and dietary fiber
| Nutrients | Function | General recommendations |
|---|---|---|
| Protein | Muscle, immunity, enzymes, satiety | Ordinary adults 0.8~1.0 g/kg; regular exercise 1.2~1.6 g/kg |
| Carbohydrates | Main energy source, brain and muscles | Depending on activity level 3~7 g/kg; endurance exercise can be higher |
| Fat | Hormones, cell membranes, fat-soluble vitamins | Accounting for 20%~35% of total energy, less trans fat |
| Dietary fiber | Intestinal tract, blood sugar, satiety | 25~30 g/day |
| Water | Body temperature, metabolism, circulation | About 1.5~2 L/day, adjusted according to sweating |

> Protein is not "more is better": People with abnormal kidney function need to follow doctor's advice; ordinary healthy people can allocate according to body weight and activity level.

### Protein source
- Animals: eggs, milk, fish, shrimp, chicken breast, lean beef, lean meat;
- Plants: soybeans and soy products, chickpeas, lentils, nuts, whole grains;
- Plant protein complement: beans + grains (such as rice + tofu) can cover essential amino acids.

## 3. Dinner plate method: a simple framework without weighing
```
½ 盘蔬菜水果
¼ 盘优质蛋白
¼ 盘主食（优先全谷物/薯类）
+ 少量健康脂肪（坚果、橄榄油、牛油果）
```
- 300~500 g of vegetables per day, half of which are dark vegetables;
- 200~350 g of fruits per day, giving priority to whole fruits rather than juice;
- Replace half of the staple food with brown rice, oats, whole wheat, corn, and sweet potatoes;
- Protein has a palm-sized amount at each meal;
- Cook with less oil and less salt, and use more steaming, boiling, stewing and roasting.

## 4. How to choose food
### 1. Priority
- Natural, less processed: vegetables, fruits, whole grains, beans, eggs, milk, fish, lean meat;
- Food with short ingredient list;
- High fiber, low added sugar, low sodium.

### 2. Control
- Sugary drinks, milk tea, fruit drinks;
- Fried foods, pastries, processed meat products;
- High-salt snacks, sauces, instant noodles;
- Alcohol (there is no "healthy amount", the less the better).

### 3. Look at the label
| What to look at | Attention |
|---|---|
| per 100 g / per serving | Confirm the unit first, don’t be misled by “per serving” |
| Ingredients List | The highest content | is at the top
| Added sugar | White sugar, fructose syrup, and maltose syrup all count |
| Sodium | More than 600 mg per 100 g is considered high |
| Fiber | Whole grains/legumes are usually higher |
| Trans fat | Be careful if there is “hydrogenated vegetable oil” in the ingredient list |

## Five, three meals and snacks
### 1. Three meals
- Breakfast: protein + staple food + fruit, such as eggs + oats + milk + banana;
- Lunch: One punch of staple food + one palm of protein + two punches of vegetables;
- Dinner: light and less oily, just eat it 2 to 3 hours before going to bed;
- There is no need to strictly "cannot eat after a certain time", the total amount and structure are more important.

### 2. Extra meals
- When you are hungry, give priority to: yogurt, nuts, fruits, hard-boiled eggs, soy milk;
- A small handful of nuts (about 20~30 g), don’t eat a whole bag while watching a show;
- can add one serving of carbohydrates + protein before and after training.

### 3. Tips for eating out
- Order vegetables and protein first, then the staple food;
- Pack the sauce separately and drink less soup base;
- Prioritize steaming, boiling, and roasting, and choose less fried, sweet and sour, and dry pot;
- For drinks, choose plain water/sugar-free tea.

## 6. Water and beverages
- White water is the best daily drink;
- Coffee/tea is okay in moderation, but be careful to add sugar and creamer;
- Sugary drinks are "liquid calories", which have poor satiety and are easy to overdose;
- If you exercise for more than 60~90 minutes or sweat a lot, consider electrolyte drinks;
- Alcohol affects sleep, recovery and judgment, so don’t drink it if you can.

## 7. How to adjust for different goals
| Goal | Method |
|---|---|
| Fat loss | Small caloric deficit + enough protein + strength training + walking |
| Muscle gain | Small caloric surplus + protein around 1.6 g/kg + progressive overload |
| Maintain | three regular meals, evenly distribute protein, weigh weekly to see trends |
| Vegetarian | Beans/soy products + whole grains + nuts, pay attention to iron, B12, calcium, Omega-3 |
| Lactose intolerance | Lactose-free milk, yogurt, cheese, or soy products for calcium supplement |

> does not recommend long-term extremely low-carb, extremely low-fat, or single-food diets; sustainability is more important than short-term weight loss.

## 8. Simple purchasing and menu for one week
### Purchase list
- Protein: eggs, chicken breast/legs, fish, shrimp, tofu, milk/yoghurt, beans;
- Staple foods: rice, oats, whole wheat bread, sweet potatoes, corn, pasta;
- Vegetables: green leafy vegetables, tomatoes, carrots, broccoli, mushrooms;
- Fruit: apples, bananas, oranges, blueberries and other seasonal fruits;
- Fat: olive oil, nuts, avocado;
- Seasoning: onion, ginger, garlic, soy sauce, vinegar, black pepper, spices.

### One day example
| meal | example |
|---|---|
| Breakfast | Oats + milk + eggs + banana |
| Lunch | Brown rice + pan-fried chicken breast + broccoli + tomato |
| Snack | Sugar-free yogurt + a small handful of nuts |
| Dinner | Sweet potatoes + steamed fish + fried vegetables + tofu soup |

## 9. Common misunderstandings
1. ❌ Carbohydrates make people fat: excess total calories is the main reason, and carbohydrates are an important source of energy;
2. ❌ Fats are not healthy: nuts, fish, and olive oil are sources of essential fatty acids;
3. ❌ You will lose weight quickly if you skip dinner: long-term total intake and sustainability are more important;
4. ❌ "Detoxification" products can cleanse the intestines: the liver and kidneys are detoxification organs themselves and do not require additional detoxification;
5. ❌ Superfoods can cure diseases: No single food can replace a balanced diet;
6. ❌ Juice is equal to fruit: Juice has less fiber and sugar is absorbed faster;
7. ❌ Eat whatever you like if it is labeled "sugar-free": it may contain sugar substitutes/starch/fat and still has calories;
8. ❌ Protein can only rely on meat: beans, milk, eggs, and grain combinations can also be used;
9. ❌ If your weight increases every day, you are fat: water and glycogen fluctuate greatly, look at the trend in 2 to 4 weeks;
10. ❌ Healthy eating must be expensive: eggs, soy products, seasonal vegetables, and staple foods are all cheap.

## 10. Summary
- First look at the total quantity and structure of ****, and then look at the details;
- Every meal has protein, vegetables, and staple food, so there won’t be much difference;
- Less processing, less sugary drinks, less alcohol, more natural foods;
- A good diet is a diet that can be adhered to for a long time.
