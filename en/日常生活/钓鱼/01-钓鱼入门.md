# Introduction to fishing
> In one sentence: Fishing is a systematic project of "finding fish + getting the fish to bite the hook + catching the fish steadily". Novices, don’t rush to buy expensive equipment. If you understand **’s compliance, safety, basic fishing groups and point selection, you will naturally catch more fish.
> Note: Different places have different regulations on fishing gear, fish species, sizes, and closed fishing periods. Please check the local fishery department/fishing ground rules before departure.

## 1. Confirm first: compliance and safety
### 1. Compliance
Does - require **fishing license/fishing license**;
Is - in the **closed fishing season/no fishing zone**;
- Which fish species have **minimum size and daily limit**;
- Whether night fishing, boat fishing, and multiple poles are allowed;
- Fishing is generally prohibited in protected areas, drinking water sources, and private waters.

### 2. Safety Checklist
| Risk | Practice |
|---|---|
| High-voltage wire | Carbon rod conducts electricity, stay away from wires, close the rod during thunderstorms |
| Falling into the water | The shore is slippery and the tide is high. Wear a life jacket when fishing on a boat |
| Fishhook | Wear glasses/hat, prepare wire cutters, and be careful when picking barbed hooks |
| Heat stroke/hypothermia | Sun protection, hydration, wearing layers in cold weather |
| Mosquitoes/ticks | Long sleeves and long pants, insect repellent, check at home |
| Traveling alone | Tell family about location and return time, bring mobile phone/power bank |
| Fish fins/gills | Wear gloves to avoid scratches and infection |

> Do not go into the water to pick up drifts or chase poles; do not stay in the dam discharge area, reef wave area, or in thunderstorm weather.

## 2. Equipment: buy "enough" first, then upgrade
### 1. Minimum List
| Category | Content | Newbie Advice |
|---|---|---|
| Rod | Hand rod or straight handle lure rod | 3.6~4.5m hand rod, or 1.8~2.1m straight handle lure rod |
| wheel | spinning wheel | 2000~3000 type, smooth force release |
| Line | Nylon/PE/Carbon Line | Hand Rod No. 1.0~2.0 Nylon; Lure PE No. 0.8~1.2 + Carbon Leader |
| hook | sleeve hook/Iseni/luya hook | Choose the size according to the target fish, prefer one size smaller |
| float | stand-up float/seven-star float | eat lead 1~2g entry-level |
| Lead | Lead skin/lead biting | Note that lead is banned in some areas |
| accessories | Figure 8 ring, connecting ring, space bean | Don’t miss small accessories |
| Tools | Hook pliers, wire cutters, net copying, towels | Net copying can significantly reduce stray fish |
| Bait | Earthworm/corn/commercial bait/luya bait | First ask local fishing friends what they use |

### 2. Budget ideas
- The first set is controlled within the acceptable range, giving priority to "the rod is not soft, the wheel is not stuck, and the line is not fake";
- Lines and hooks are the cheapest consumables and are also the most common sources of missing fish, so they should be replaced regularly;
- Don’t blindly pursue “one pole to conquer the world”: hand pole, lure, and sea fishing are different methods of fishing.

## 3. Common fishing methods and fishing groups
### 1. Hand pole fishing/suspended fishing
```
竿稍 → 主线 → 太空豆 → 漂座 → 铅皮座 → 八字环 → 子线 → 双钩
```
- Adjust the lead sheet to allow the float to reveal the target mesh number;
- Fishing for the bottom, fishing for float, and fishing off the bottom are controlled by "finding the bottom" and "adjusting the float";
- is suitable for freshwater fish such as crucian carp, carp, and tilapia.

### 2. Lure
- uses fake bait to imitate small fish/insects and induces attacks by reeling in the line;
- Common baits: glitter, minnow, soft worm, VIB, thunder frog;
- Target fish: bass, cockhead, snakehead, horsemouth, etc.;
- The key is to "find the structure + control the swimming layer + variable speed reeling", not to reel in all the time.

### 3. Sea fishing/rock fishing
- is greatly affected by tides, wave conditions, and rocks, and has the highest safety requirements;
- is commonly used for floating rock fishing, bottom fishing and iron plate fishing;
- You must pay attention to tide tables, wind and wave forecasts, and local no-fishing zones.

### 4. Ice fishing
- is only carried out in areas where the ice thickness is confirmed to be safe;
- requires ice auger, ice fishing rod, and thermal insulation equipment;
- It is not recommended for novices to ice fish alone.

## 4. Selection: Where are the fish?
### 1. Find the structure
- Water grass edges, reed seams, fallen trees, bridge piers, and rocky areas;
- The junction of deep and shallow, water inlet, backwater bay, downwind outlet;
- Look for shade/deep water in summer, and sunny/deep water in winter;
- On rainy days, the dissolved oxygen at the water inlet is high and fish often gather.

### 2. Check the weather
| Conditions | General rules |
|---|---|
| Early morning/evening | High fish activity, window period |
| Cloudy/light rain | The light is weak and fish dare not come near |
| Sudden drop in air pressure/stifling heat | Fish mouth may become worse |
| After the cold air passes | It is difficult to fish for a short time, wait until it stabilizes |
| Strong wind | Food gathers in the downwind, but casting is difficult |

> Weather patterns are only for reference. Different fishing positions in the same water area may have greater differences; observe more and change points more often.

## 5. Hook binding and basic operations
### 1. Two enough knots
- **Improved Clinch Knot**: Thread the eye of the hook → Wind the thread 5~7 times → Thread the thread back into the small loop near the eye of the hook → Moisten → Tighten;
- **Paloma knot**: Fold the thread through the eye of the hook → tie a loose knot → pass the hook through the loop → moisten and tighten.
> Be sure to **** before tightening to avoid friction and damage to the thread; after pulling, pull the hook to confirm that it is not slippery.

### 2. Throwing rods and stabbing fish
- Hand Rod: Throw lightly in place, do not whip the whip; reduce the throwing range when the wind is strong;
- Lure: Use the slight elasticity of the rod and use your thumb to control the line cup before falling into the water;
- When you see a pause/black drift, **use your wrist to stab the fish in short bursts.** do not swing the rod sharply;
- After hitting the fish, keep the rod body bent and use force release/walking the fish to consume energy. Do not pull hard.

### 3. Copy fish
- After the fish turns white, you can catch it. After the net enters the water, catch it from the direction of the fish's head;
- Do not lift the fish directly to the shore with a pole, as it may break the pole/detach the hook easily.

## 6. Fish handling and release
- Fish retention: Bleeding/preserving freshness as soon as possible according to local quota and avoiding exposure to the sun;
- Release: Operate with wet hands, do not pinch the gills, and do not stay out of the water for a long time;
- Trim the thread when it is difficult to remove the hook, do not pull hard;
- Put the fish back into the water, straighten it up, and wait until it has strength before letting go;
- Alien invasive fish species should be handled according to local regulations and should not be released at will.

## 7. Frequently asked questions for newbies
| Problem | Cause | Solution |
|---|---|---|
| Throwing explosive line | The line cup brake is too small and the bait is too light | Increase the brake and practice casting with the wind |
| The thread is rolled up | The thread is too old and the reel takes up the thread too loosely | Change the thread and go online again |
| The float does not move | The fishing rig is not at the bottom/the water depth is wrong | Find the bottom again and adjust the float |
| Missing the mouth | The hook is too big, the bait is too hard, and the rod is lifted too early/too late | Change to a small hook, soft bait, and release the mouth |
| The middle fish runs away | The hook is not sharp, the line is damaged, and the leakage force is too tight | Change the hook, line, and loosen the leakage force |
| There is no mouth. | The fishing position/swimming layer/bait are wrong. | Change the point, depth and bait. |
| Hanging to the bottom | The line retraction in the structure area is too slow | Raise the rod slightly, change bait, abandon the hook to protect the line |

## 8. Minimum entry-level solution
1. Check local rules first to confirm that fishing is allowed;
2. Buy a set of 3.6~4.5m hand pole + line set + float + hook + net;
3. Find a nearby park lake/charged fishing spot that allows fishing, and practice drift adjustment and rod throwing first;
4. The target fish starts from crucian carp/tilapia, and the bait is earthworms or commonly used local bait;
5. records every time: weather, fishing position, water depth, bait, fish mouth, and accumulates own experience.

## 9. Common misunderstandings
1. ❌ The more expensive the equipment, the more fish you will get: fishing position, weather, and bait status are more important than the price of the rod;
2. ❌ The larger the hook, the less likely it is for fish to escape: the target fish is small, but a large hook will block the fish;
3. ❌ If you have no mouth, you will die and wait: take the initiative to change points, change swimming layers, and change bait;
4. ❌ Raise the rod vigorously when there is a mouth: it is easy to pull the fish's mouth open and break the line;
5. ❌ Releasing means throwing it back into the water: Improper operation will greatly reduce the survival rate;
6. ❌ You can fish anywhere: it may be illegal in no-fishing areas, protected areas, and private waters;
7. ❌ "The fish mouth is good" during thunderstorms: Carbon rod + waterside is an extremely high-risk combination, so close the rod immediately.

## 10. Summary
- fishing = **compliance + safety + finding fish + fishing group + operation**;
- Beginners should first practice the four basic movements of "adjusting the drift, throwing the rod, stabbing the fish, and copying the fish";
- More records and more observations will make progress faster than blindly buying equipment;
- Only by taking away trash, observing limits, and treating fish well will you always have fish to catch.
